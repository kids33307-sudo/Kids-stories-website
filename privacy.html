Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy describes the proposed Kids Stories Automation tool, designed for adult creators to prepare and publish original educational animated videos using their own authorized social-media accounts. The tool is still being configured; descriptions of connected features below apply only when those features are enabled.

Who can use it

The tool is for adult content managers, not for children. It does not provide child sign-ups or request information directly from children.

Information used

With account-owner permission, a publishing integration may access the account name or ID, basic profile fields, authorization credentials, permitted video content, captions and publishing status. If you contact the project by email, the contents of your message and reply address may be processed to answer your request.

Purposes

Information is used to authenticate authorized account connections, prepare requested posts, submit approved videos to the selected service, track their status, troubleshoot failures and maintain security. Information is not sold for advertising.

Sharing

When a creator requests publication, the video and necessary related information are sent to the selected platform, which has its own privacy terms. Service providers may process limited data to host or operate the integration where required.

Storage

Any deployed integration should keep credentials on a restricted server and not expose them in a public repository. Connection information should be held only while needed to operate the service, with reasonable safeguards. This website does not assert that a publishing backend has been deployed.

Access and deletion

You may revoke account authorization through the connected platform. To ask about your data or request its deletion, email kids33307@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will consider applicable requests and reply within a reasonable time, subject to any legal retention requirements.

Changes

This notice may change as features are deployed. We will update the effective date when material changes are published.
